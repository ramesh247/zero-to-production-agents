# 08 · Choosing a model: quality vs latency vs cost

Four Claude models (plus Haiku 5.5 with thinking off), three tasks from earlier builds, every answer checked against a known value. The question isn't "which model is best", it's "which is cheapest that passes, for this task".

**What you'll learn**
- Why every model scored **60/60** on ticket extraction, at prices from **$0.10 to $3.82 per 1,000 tickets**
- How the cheapest model tied Opus **30/30** on counting questions, and dropped to **5/30** with thinking off
- Why the newer models count the same text as **31% more tokens**, and what that does to price comparisons

## Quickstart
Needs an Anthropic API key. A full run cost **$0.98**, almost all of it Sonnet and Opus. A budget guard stops it at $4.
```sh
git clone https://github.com/ramesh247/zero-to-production-agents.git

```

## Results from a real run (each model at its default settings)

| Setup | Tickets: objective (of 60) | Tickets: agrees with my labels (of 60) | Invoice at 300×375 (of 62, 3 runs) | Catalog counting (of 30) | $ per 1,000 tickets | Cost of all 26 calls |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Haiku 4.5 | 60 | 51 | 4-8 | 4 | $0.80 | $0.053 |
| Haiku 5.5 | 60 | 52 | 54-62 | **30** | **$0.10** | $0.025 |
| Haiku 5.5, thinking off | 60 | 53 | 56-57 | 5 | $0.10 | $0.007 |
| Sonnet 5.5 | 60 | 52 | 59-62 | 30 | $1.91 | $0.328 |
| Opus 5.5 | 60 | 51 | **62** (every run) | 30 | $3.82 | $0.564 |

**Catalog counting, per call:** Haiku 5.5 $0.0067 and 38.9 s median (11,295 output tokens of thinking and answer) | Sonnet 5.5 $0.0815, 33.2 s | Opus 5.5 $0.1305, 33.5 s | Haiku 5.5 thinking off $0.0012, 1.6 s, but 5/30.

**Default settings matter:** Haiku 4.5 doesn't think unless asked. Haiku 5.5, Sonnet 5.5 and Opus 5.5 think by default (Opus 5.5 can't turn it off), and thinking is billed as output.

**Tokenizer:** the same catalog was 8,325 input tokens on Haiku 4.5 and 10,916 on the three newer models.
